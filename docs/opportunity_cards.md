## C1. Frozen Pizza / lapsing / digital coupon (REJECTED for funding)
- **Strategy class:** coupon / paid contact · **Evidence level:** OBSERVED behaviour + ASSUMED economics
- **Segment / reach:** {'panel_households_lapsing_at_53': 406, 'reachability': 'UNKNOWN (consent, offer mechanics)', 'substantial': 'UNKNOWN at a real company (7,308 is ASSUMED)'}
- **Observed:** return to category H1→H2 80% of H1 buyers; 61% of the lapsing cohort is new each 4 weeks; 34% of category sales occur while on display or in the mailer (association); model ranks lapsing buyers better than recency (AP 0.42 vs 0.35), but ranking ≠ persuasion
- **Counterevidence:** every modelled coupon plan loses at base (best Frozen Pizza plan -1,824 per 4-week cycle, assumed scale); flips only under joint changes (e.g., manufacturer-funded coupon + larger reachable pool), none confirmed
- **Evidence that would decide:** interviews: price vs habit vs stock vs switching; who funds the coupon; consented pool size; randomized response
- **Hypothesized need:** an easy dinner restarted after a lapse (HYPOTHESIS)
- **Positioning (to test):** the easy dinner you already like, back on your list (to test, not to publish)
- **Success metric:** incremental contribution per randomized household above the rollout hurdle (pilot analyzer)
- **4 Ps:** Product: as is; Price: coupon only if price is the barrier; Place: check stock/pickup first; Promotion: reminder before discount
- **Recommendation:** do not fund now; research first

## C2. Basket attachment: BAG SNACKS → FROZEN PIZZA
- **Strategy class:** place / merchandising (non-discount) · **Evidence level:** OBSERVED association, WEAK (negligible margin over a popularity suggestion); effect UNKNOWN
- **Segment / reach:** {'baskets_with_A_H2': 12412, 'reachability': "in-store placement or app 'goes well with' module; no individual contact needed"}
- **Observed:** lift 2.521 (H1) and 2.493 (H2, CI [2.407, 2.58]) — survives the held-out half; P(FROZEN PIZZA|BAG SNACKS) 20.6% vs popularity benchmark COLD CEREAL 20.5% in H2
- **Counterevidence:** co-purchase can reflect shared shopping missions (association, not causation); only 1 of 56 tested pairs both survive the held-out half and beat simply suggesting the most popular category; here the margin is 0.06 pp, i.e. negligible; most basket 'lift' among these categories reflects large shopping trips, not specific affinity; no data on whether adjacency or a bundle would add incremental baskets
- **Evidence that would decide:** store placement test with a holdout of stores (operations validation); interviews on shopping missions
- **Hypothesized need:** convenience: complete a meal/occasion in one trip (HYPOTHESIS)
- **Positioning (to test):** frozen pizza that goes with your bag snacks
- **Success metric:** incremental share of BAG SNACKS baskets that also contain FROZEN PIZZA vs control stores
- **4 Ps:** Product: none; Price: no discount needed; Place: adjacency / secondary display / app pairing; Promotion: in-store or in-app suggestion
- **Recommendation:** low priority: weak evidence; consider only as a zero-cost app 'goes well with' default after operations sign-off

## C3. YOGURT: shrinking buyer base
- **Strategy class:** product / place investigation (non-discount) · **Evidence level:** OBSERVED trend; cause UNKNOWN
- **Segment / reach:** {'buyers_H1': 1196, 'buyers_H2': 1136, 'reachability': 'assortment/stock review; no contact needed'}
- **Observed:** buyers -5.0% H2 vs H1 while most categories grew; return to category 70%; private label 22%→22% (stable)
- **Counterevidence:** seasonality cannot be separated with one year of data; no stock, price-list or competitor data; robustness: on equal 25-week windows buyers change -7.0% (buyer rate 49.9% -> 46.1% of active households); not panel attrition: 353 of 368 lost buyers still shop in the later window; active households 2,391 -> 2,410; not a promotion or coverage change: promoted product-store rows/week 9,757.3 -> 9,718.1; sales share on promotion 19.2% -> 21.1%; lines in covered stores 98.2% -> 99.0%; verdict: a SIGNAL for an operations check, not a diagnosed cause
- **Evidence that would decide:** operations: stock-out and assortment changes in H2; interviews with lapsed buyers; second year of data for seasonality
- **Hypothesized need:** availability / assortment fit (HYPOTHESIS: could equally be seasonal)
- **Positioning (to test):** n/a until the barrier is known
- **Success metric:** buyers per 4 weeks returns to H1 level after the fix (vs same weeks last year when available)
- **4 Ps:** Product: assortment review; Price: check price moves (no price list in data); Place: stock-out / shelf check; Promotion: none until diagnosed
- **Recommendation:** operational investigation; no spend
